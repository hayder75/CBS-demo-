**Applies to:** Anchor Financials offices, server rooms, hosting facilities, devices and staff working on or off site.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 To prevent unauthorized physical access, damage, interference, theft or loss affecting Anchor CBS, its data and supporting equipment.

1.2 To ensure power, cooling, fire protection and environmental conditions support reliable service.

## 2. Scope

2.1 Anchor CBS production infrastructure is hosted at the contracted hosting provider. This policy applies to the provider's data-centre controls, any Anchor Financials server room or network closet, branch and office areas, and all devices used to access Anchor CBS.

2.2 Where a control is operated by the hosting provider, Anchor Financials verifies it through contract terms, assurance reports and periodic assessment. Accountability remains with Anchor Financials.

## 3. Hosting provider and data-centre controls

3.1 The hosting provider must maintain:

- controlled entry with badge, biometric or equivalent authentication;
- 24/7 monitoring and response;
- visitor authorization, escort and logging;
- CCTV coverage of entries, aisles and equipment areas;
- fire detection and suppression;
- redundant power, UPS and generator or equivalent;
- temperature, humidity and water-leak monitoring;
- secure media storage and destruction;
- protection against unauthorized cabling or equipment connection.

3.2 Anchor Financials reviews the provider's assurance evidence annually and records the result. Gaps require compensating controls or a remediation plan under AF-POL-09.

3.3 The provider must notify Anchor Financials of physical security incidents, unauthorized access attempts and planned maintenance that could affect service.

3.4 Production data must not be hosted in an unapproved location. Cross-border hosting requires approval under AF-POL-06.

## 4. Access authorization

4.1 Access to secure areas is limited to named personnel with a documented business need.

4.2 Access is approved by the Operations Manager and reviewed at least annually. Access is removed immediately when the need ends or employment terminates.

4.3 Access cards, keys and codes are personal, not shared, and are returned on leaving. Lost cards are deactivated immediately.

4.4 A current access list is maintained for each secure area. The list is reconciled with HR records quarterly.

4.5 Vendor technicians require a written request, approval and escort. Their access is time-limited and recorded.

## 5. Visitors

5.1 Visitors sign in and out at reception, provide identification, state their host and purpose, and wear a visitor badge.

5.2 Visitors are escorted in secure areas at all times. They must not connect devices, access systems or photograph equipment without approval.

5.3 Visitor logs record name, organization, host, purpose, time in/out and badge number. They are retained for two years, or longer if related to an incident.

5.4 Deliveries are received at a controlled loading area and inspected before being brought into secure areas.

## 6. CCTV and monitoring

6.1 CCTV covers entrances, exits, secure-area corridors and equipment rooms where lawful and proportionate.

6.2 Cameras are positioned to avoid private areas. Signage informs staff and visitors of monitoring.

6.3 Recordings are retained for 30 days, unless required for an incident or investigation, and access is restricted to authorized security and operations staff.

6.4 Recordings are disclosed only to law enforcement or under legal authority, with CEO or Legal approval.

6.5 CCTV systems are tested and maintained, and time is synchronized.

## 7. Power and environmental controls

7.1 Production equipment is protected by UPS with sufficient runtime for orderly shutdown or generator start, and by surge protection.

7.2 UPS and generator systems are tested at least quarterly. Test results are recorded.

7.3 Temperature, humidity and water ingress are monitored with alarms. Conditions outside approved ranges trigger a response.

7.4 Fire detection and suppression systems are maintained and inspected according to the provider's schedule and local requirements.

7.5 Cabling is protected, labelled and separated to prevent accidental damage or interception.

7.6 Maintenance activities are scheduled, authorized and recorded and must not bypass security controls without approval.

## 8. Secure disposal

8.1 Equipment containing data (servers, disks, laptops, phones, printers, copiers, removable media) is sanitized before disposal, reuse, repair or transfer.

8.2 Sanitization uses approved methods consistent with NIST SP 800-88: cryptographic erasure, secure overwrite or physical destruction.

8.3 Paper containing personal, financial or confidential data is cross-cut shredded or destroyed by an approved vendor, with a certificate of destruction.

8.4 Disposal is authorized by the Operations Manager and recorded in an asset disposal log. Certificates are retained for seven years.

8.5 Devices are not sold, donated or discarded with storage intact. Asset tags and data-bearing components are accounted for.

## 9. Laptop and device controls

9.1 Organization laptops and mobile devices use full-disk encryption, screen locks, endpoint protection and current patches.

9.2 Devices are registered to a named user and recorded in the asset register. Unregistered devices may not connect to the network.

9.3 Devices are stored securely when not in use. Laptops are not left visible in vehicles or public areas.

9.4 Loss or theft is reported immediately to the CISO/Security Officer and treated as an incident under AF-POL-03. Access is revoked and the device is remotely wiped where possible.

9.5 Repairs and maintenance are performed by approved providers. Data is backed up and, where practicable, removed before repair.

9.6 USB and removable media restrictions are governed by AF-POL-07.

## 10. Office and branch security

10.1 Secure areas are locked when unattended. Keys and access cards are controlled and logged.

10.2 Reception and branch counters are arranged so that screens and documents are not visible to members or visitors.

10.3 Cash and negotiable instruments are protected under separate finance procedures; this policy covers their information and system aspects only.

10.4 Staff must challenge unescorted strangers in secure areas and report suspicious persons or activity.

10.5 Clean-desk and clear-screen rules apply under AF-POL-07.

## 11. Remote work

11.1 Remote access to Anchor CBS production is permitted only for approved roles and with organization-managed devices.

11.2 Remote access uses the approved VPN or secure gateway with MFA under AF-POL-02.

11.3 Staff must use a private, lockable space, avoid public Wi-Fi without protection, and prevent shoulder surfing or overhearing of member data.

11.4 Member and financial data must not be printed, stored locally or removed from approved systems when working remotely.

11.5 Remote workers must report loss, theft, intrusion or suspected compromise immediately.

## 12. Incident reporting and review

12.1 Physical security incidents (unauthorized access, theft, damage, power failure, environmental alarm, lost device) are reported immediately to the Operations Manager and CISO/Security Officer, and handled under AF-POL-03.

12.2 The site is preserved for investigation where appropriate.

12.3 This policy is reviewed annually, and after a physical incident, relocation, new hosting arrangement or audit finding. A site assessment is conducted at least annually.
