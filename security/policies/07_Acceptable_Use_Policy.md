**Applies to:** All employees, directors, contractors, interns, temporary staff and third parties who use Anchor Financials systems, devices, networks or data.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 To define acceptable and prohibited use of Anchor Financials information systems, so that use is professional, lawful, secure and consistent with Anchor CBS operations.

1.2 To protect members, staff and the organization from fraud, data loss, malware and reputational harm.

## 2. Authorized use

2.1 Access is provided for approved business purposes. Limited personal use of email and internet is permitted where it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, breach law or create risk.

2.2 Users must follow their role permissions, approval limits and segregation-of-duties rules under AF-POL-02.

2.3 Users must complete required security awareness training and acknowledge this policy before receiving an account.

2.4 Users must keep credentials confidential, lock their workstation when unattended, and report loss, theft or suspected compromise immediately.

2.5 Users must verify the identity and authority of anyone requesting data, payments or changes, and must not bypass maker-checker controls.

## 3. Prohibited activities

3.1 The following are prohibited:

- sharing accounts, passwords or OTPs, or allowing another person to use a logged-in session;
- accessing data without a business need, or browsing member accounts out of curiosity;
- altering, deleting or concealing records, audit logs or evidence;
- processing transactions outside approved limits or without required approval;
- installing unauthorized software, browser extensions or utilities;
- connecting unauthorized devices, USB storage or personal media to the network;
- using personal email, personal cloud storage or messaging apps for member or financial data;
- sending phishing, spam, chain messages or offensive material;
- attempting to bypass security controls, scan or penetrate systems without written authorization;
- using systems for fraud, theft, money laundering, unauthorized lending or personal gain;
- copying, photographing or screen-recording member or financial data without approval;
- using another person's credentials or accessing their mailbox or files without authorization;
- any activity that breaches Ethiopian law, NBE directives or the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

3.2 Suspected fraud or theft is reported under AF-POL-03 and may be referred to police.

## 4. Devices and network use

4.1 Only organization-owned or explicitly approved devices may access production systems. Devices must run supported software, current patches, endpoint protection and full-disk encryption.

4.2 Users must not disable antivirus, firewalls, screen locks, logging or device management controls.

4.3 Laptops and mobile devices must be locked when unattended and stored securely when travelling. Loss or theft is reported immediately.

4.4 Personal devices may access email or collaboration tools only through approved services with MFA. Personal devices may not store member or financial data.

4.5 Removable media is prohibited unless approved and encrypted by the CISO/Security Officer for a specific purpose.

4.6 Users must connect to production only through approved networks and VPN, never public Wi-Fi without protection.

4.7 Anchor CBS workstations must not be left logged in at branch counters or shared areas.

## 5. Email and internet

5.1 Users must exercise caution with attachments, links and unexpected requests. Payment or data requests that appear urgent must be verified by phone using a known number.

5.2 Phishing and suspicious email must be reported to [Email] and not forwarded to colleagues.

5.3 Email must not be used to send passwords, OTPs, full card or account credentials, or unencrypted bulk personal data.

5.4 Internet access is monitored and filtered. Attempts to access malicious, illegal or inappropriate sites are blocked and reviewed.

5.5 Users are responsible for the content they send and must use approved signatures and disclaimers where required.

## 6. Data handling

6.1 Member, KYC, financial and personnel data is classified Internal or Confidential and must be accessed only on a need-to-know basis.

6.2 Data must not be stored on personal drives, personal email, unapproved cloud services, USB sticks or local desktops.

6.3 Approved shared repositories must have named access, encryption and backup. Exports require owner approval and must be deleted when the purpose ends.

6.4 Paper records containing personal or financial data must be locked away, transported securely and shredded when no longer needed under AF-POL-05.

6.5 Screens showing member or financial data must not be visible to unauthorized persons. Privacy filters are used in public-facing areas where required.

6.6 Data must be masked or redacted in reports and screenshots where full values are unnecessary.

6.7 Data must not be taken home, copied for personal use or used for training or testing without approval and masking.

## 7. Clean desk and clear screen

7.1 At the end of each day, desks must be clear of documents containing personal, financial or confidential information. Such documents are locked in approved storage.

7.2 Whiteboards and notice boards must not display member or financial data.

7.3 Screens lock after 10 minutes of inactivity and users must lock manually when leaving. Printouts are collected immediately.

7.4 Keys, tokens and access cards must not be left unattended or shared.

## 8. Reporting

8.1 Users must report suspected incidents, phishing, malware, data loss, fraud, policy violations and control weaknesses immediately to the CISO/Security Officer at [Phone] / [Email] or the service desk at [Phone] / [Email].

8.2 Reports made in good faith are protected. Users will not be penalized for reporting their own mistake promptly.

8.3 Deliberate concealment of a breach or failure to report is itself a disciplinary matter.

## 9. Monitoring and privacy

9.1 Anchor Financials monitors systems, networks, email, access and activity for security, compliance, fraud prevention and operational purposes.

9.2 By using Anchor Financials systems, users consent to monitoring and logging. Monitoring is conducted in accordance with Ethiopian law and the Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

9.3 Monitoring is proportionate and access to monitoring data is restricted to authorized personnel. Personal communications are not inspected unless necessary for an investigation and authorized by the CEO or Legal.

9.4 Users must have no expectation of privacy in organization-owned systems, devices, accounts or data.

9.5 Monitoring records are handled under AF-POL-05 and AF-POL-11.

## 10. Consequences

10.1 Violations may result in withdrawal of access, corrective action, formal warning, disciplinary action up to termination, recovery of losses, and referral to law enforcement.

10.2 Contractors may have their contract suspended or terminated and may be removed from site.

10.3 Consequences are applied consistently with Human Resources policy and Ethiopian labour law.

## 11. Acknowledgement and review

11.1 All users sign an acknowledgement before account activation and annually thereafter. Acknowledgement records are kept by Human Resources for five years.

11.2 This policy is reviewed annually and after a significant incident or change in law.
